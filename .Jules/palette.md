## 2024-06-05 - Contextual Empty States
**Learning:** Hardcoding technical terms like "No data" in reusable empty states creates friction when the empty state represents a positive or neutral condition (like "Inbox Zero", "Queue clear", or "Setup").
**Action:** Always make empty state eyebrows/labels contextual or optional so they can match the emotional valence of the state (e.g., success vs. setup vs. missing).
